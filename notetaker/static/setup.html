Welcome — let's get you set up

Everything runs on this machine. Three steps, about a minute plus a one-time model download.

1 · License & attributionpending

Notetaker uses the Bodhan indic-transcribe model (Indic Open Model License: attribution + share-alike), sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0) with pyannote segmentation (CC-BY-NC-4.0 — personal/non-commercial use) and 3D-Speaker embeddings, and Noto fonts (OFL). Audio and notes never leave this device. By continuing you accept these terms for local use.

2 · Modelschecking…

One-time download: int4 speech model (~700 MB) + diarization models (~45 MB). Stored locally; afterwards Notetaker works fully offline.

Optional — code-mix model (flex, ~4.6 GB): keeps English words in English ("Linux operating system" instead of "लिनक्स ऑपरेटिंग सिस्टम"). Gated on Hugging Face: accept its license there and sign in with hf auth login first.
